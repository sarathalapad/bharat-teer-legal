Privacy Policy · भारत तीर

Bharat Teer: Arrow Puzzle by Sarathkumar.K
Android package com.eaop.bharatteer
Effective date: 9 November 2026 (version 4)

In short: Bharat Teer is a free puzzle game for ages 13 and over, supported by ads from Google AdMob. Sarathkumar.K itself does not collect, receive or sell your personal information. The Google ad SDK in the app does collect and share some device data with Google to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • No data collected by Sarathkumar.K
  • Progress stays on your device
  • No in-app purchases
  • No accounts
  • Rewarded ads are optional
  • Consent choice for EEA/UK users

1. Information we (Sarathkumar.K) collect

None. Sarathkumar.K does not ask for, collect or receive your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and we do not run any servers that receive data from the app. We do not use any analytics service of our own (Unity analytics is disabled).

The only third party that receives data from the app is Google, through the advertising SDK described below.

2. Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves a small file only on your own device, inside the app's private storage. It contains:

  • game progress (levels completed, stars, coins, hints, hearts, Daily Puzzle streak), and
  • settings (language, sound, music, vibration and graphics preferences).

This file is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see or access it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app; uninstalling removes all of it.

3. Internet and network access

The game needs an internet connection to start (it only checks that a connection works and sends no personal data for that check). Otherwise the app uses the internet only for ads: to load and show ads, to ask for and store your ad consent choice (where required), and for the ad SDK's measurement and fraud-prevention reporting.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Bharat Teer shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. The app may show:

  • app-open ads when you open or return to the game,
  • interstitial (full-screen) ads after every level (on the result screen, never during play),
  • a banner ad at the bottom of the menu and level-map screens (never during play), and
  • rewarded ads that are always optional: you can choose to watch an ad to get a free hint, double coins or an extra heart. If you do not want to watch, simply skip the offer.

We ask Google to show only ads suitable for teens and up (maximum ad content rating PG). Tapping an ad may open a web page or the Google Play Store outside the game.

Google's use of data from apps that use its services is described here:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data for advertising and AdMob privacy and messaging information

6. Your consent choices (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalised ads are used. You can:

  • consent to personalised ads, or
  • decline / manage options, in which case Google shows non-personalised (or limited) ads, which still use some data for frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention, as allowed by law.

You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings > Ad privacy choices. Where other privacy laws (for example some US state laws) give you similar rights, Google's ad controls and the options below apply.

7. Advertising ID: reset or opt out

You can control the Advertising ID on your phone at any time in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact menu name varies by phone):

  • Reset advertising ID - gives you a new random ID, or
  • Delete advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalisation - apps and ad networks can then no longer use the ID for personalised ads.

You can also manage Google ad personalisation at myadcenter.google.com.

8. Purchases and accounts

There are no in-app purchases and no accounts, sign-in, chat or social features. Coins, hints and hearts in the game are earned by playing (or by optionally watching a rewarded ad) and have no real-money value.

9. Sharing of information

Sarathkumar.K does not sell, rent or share personal information. The only data that leaves your device is the data the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google (and, through Google's ad systems, with ad partners as described in Google's policies) for the purposes in section 5.

Rating and sharing. Settings > Rate us opens the game's Google Play page, and the game may occasionally show Google Play's own in-app review card after you complete a level. Any rating or review you write is sent to and published by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy; the game does not receive it or learn whether you left one. Share game opens Android's share menu with a short message and the Google Play link; you choose the app and the people you share with, and nothing is sent unless you send it.

10. Permissions

The app uses these Android permissions:

  • Vibration - optional haptic feedback when you tap arrows (can be turned off in game settings).
  • Internet and network state - added by the ad SDK to load ads.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) - added by the ad SDK to read the Advertising ID (see section 7 to reset or delete it).

11. Children

Bharat Teer is not designed for or directed at children under 13. Its target audience is people aged 13 and over, although its content is family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child under 13 is using the app, please contact us at the email below. We will help, for example by explaining how to reset or delete the Advertising ID on the device and how to remove the app and its data. Because Sarathkumar.K holds no personal data, any ad data is held by Google; you can also use Google's controls listed in sections 4 and 7.

12. Security

Game progress stays on your device in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandboxing. Data sent by the ad SDK travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

13. Your rights and deleting data

Data protection laws (such as the EU/UK GDPR and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) give you rights such as access, correction and deletion of personal data.

  • Data held by Sarathkumar.K: none, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete.
  • Game progress on your device: delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
  • Ad data held by Google: reset or delete your Advertising ID (section 7), change consent in Settings > Ad privacy choices (section 6), and use Google's tools at myadcenter.google.com and policies.google.com/privacy.

If you need help with any request, contact us and we will assist you.

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date and version number above and publish the new version on this page. We will also update the Google Play Data safety section before releasing any version of the game that changes what data is collected.
